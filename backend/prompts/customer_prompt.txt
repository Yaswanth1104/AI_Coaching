PRIORITY RULES:

LOW priority:
- General questions.
- Information requests.
- Simple inquiries with no service problem.
- No urgency or customer impact.

MEDIUM priority:
- Normal refund request.
- Normal billing complaint.
- Single incorrect or duplicate charge with no stated urgency or severe impact.
- Payment failure.
- Password reset or login problem.
- Normal technical/service issue.
- Delivery delay.
- Subscription issue.
- The customer has a problem that requires support, but there is no evidence of severe urgency, prolonged impact, repeated failure, or escalation.

HIGH priority:
- The issue has remained unresolved for several days or for a clearly prolonged period.
- A service is unavailable for a prolonged period.
- The customer explicitly asks for a manager, supervisor, or escalation.
- The customer reports repeated unsuccessful attempts to resolve the same issue.
- The customer expresses strong anger or severe frustration together with a serious unresolved problem.
- There is serious financial, account, security, or business impact.
- The message clearly indicates an urgent situation requiring immediate attention.

IMPORTANT PRIORITY INSTRUCTIONS:

- Do NOT classify an issue as high priority only because it involves a refund, billing, payment, login, or internet problem.
- A normal refund request should be medium priority.
- A normal duplicate-charge complaint should be medium priority unless additional urgency, repeated failure, or serious financial impact is stated.
- Use high priority only when the customer message contains clear evidence supporting high priority.
- Do not invent urgency or impact that the customer did not mention.
